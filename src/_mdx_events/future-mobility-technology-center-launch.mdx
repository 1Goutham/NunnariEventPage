## The launch

KGiSL Institute of Technology and Thoughtworks signed a memorandum of understanding to unveil the Future Mobility Technology Center on the KITE campus in Coimbatore. The centre is dedicated to software-defined vehicles, including autonomous and self-driving cars, and to research in intelligent mobility more broadly.

## Who said what

Dr. Ashok Bakthavathsalam, Managing Trustee at KITE, called the partnership a significant step in advancing SDV technology within India and a unique opportunity for students. Vanya Seth, Head of Technology at Thoughtworks India, spoke about blending industry innovation with academic excellence and pushing the boundaries of connected, autonomous, shared and electrified vehicles.

## Our role

Navaneeth was institutional lead for the launch as Director of Innovation at KGiSL.
