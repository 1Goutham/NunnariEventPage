## The project

Team KITE / KGX from KGiSL Institute of Technology prepared for the F1Tenth autonomous racing competition, run with the Autoware Foundation and the F1Tenth Foundation. The team started with a radio-controlled car and finished with a fully autonomous vehicle running on a track mapped with LiDAR.

## What the car does

- **Wall follow**: maintains a safe distance from walls and obstacles while navigating.
- **Gap follow**: assesses the space between vehicles, finds safe gaps and follows the leader.
- **Autonomous emergency braking**: applies the brakes automatically when sensors detect a collision hazard.

## Why it is here

This is where our Physical AI practice comes from. The discipline of getting perception, planning and control to work on a real vehicle with real latency and real failure modes is the same discipline we bring to factory floors and edge devices today.
