## The meetup

The first FOSS United meetup in Coimbatore, hosted at Thoughtworks in KCT Tech Park. Navaneeth shared the stage with Raju Kandaswamy, with thanks to Venkatesh Hariharan and Satish Kumar Viswanathan for bringing the open-source spirit to the city.

## The talk

*The Open Source AI Revolution: Democratising Innovation and Fostering Responsible Development.*

- How open source is democratising AI and driving innovation.
- The role of diversity and inclusivity in who gets to build these systems.
- Balancing open-source contribution with commercial interest to keep AI development responsible.
