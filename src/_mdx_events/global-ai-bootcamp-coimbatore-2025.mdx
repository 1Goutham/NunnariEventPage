## The event

The Global AI Bootcamp is a free, full-day community event with hands-on workshops and expert sessions. The 2025 Coimbatore edition was hosted at Presidio in Saravanampatti, with sessions on small language models for domain reasoning, data engineering on Azure Databricks, Microsoft 365 Copilot agents and productionising GenAI applications.

Nunnari Labs was there in a supporting role. A healthy AI community in Coimbatore needs more than one organiser, and we show up for the others.
