## The league

KGiSL Educational Institutions organised a DeepRacer league so students and developers could start their machine learning journey in a fun way: train a reinforcement learning model, then race it on a physical track. The track was launched on campus in December 2021, a three-activity workshop series ran through February, and the main event took place on 10 and 11 March 2022, with AI Coimbatore as community partner.

This is the earliest event in the archive where a car drives itself on a track we built. The Physical AI practice grew from here.
