## The league

KGiSL Educational Institutions organised a DeepRacer league so students and developers could start their machine learning journey in a fun way: train a reinforcement learning model, then race it on a physical track. The track was launched on campus in December 2021, a three-activity workshop series ran through February, and the main event took place on 10 and 11 March 2022. The poster lists AI Coimbatore as community partner.
