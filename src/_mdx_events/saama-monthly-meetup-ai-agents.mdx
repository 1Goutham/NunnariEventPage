## The session

Saama's monthly meetup in Coimbatore, with a technical session on AI agents: how they work, their key principles, and the architecture that powers them. How agents interact with their environment, make decisions, and execute tasks autonomously toward a goal.

## What we covered

- What a generative AI agent actually is, and what separates it from a chatbot.
- Multi-agent architectures, where several agents collaborate toward a common objective.
- Building agents with LangChain and LangGraph, and where those frameworks help and hinder.

One question raised in an attendee's recap: how can reinforcement learning and other optimisation methods be used to continuously enhance agent performance and enable adaptation to new data?
