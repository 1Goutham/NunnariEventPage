## The session

A hands-on AI workshop for Landcom's technology team, led with our team at DeepWeaver.ai. What stuck with us was not the tooling. It was watching a room of capable, careful people move from cautious experimentation to confident, governed use of AI on real work, in a single day.

## Why it matters

Most enterprise AI does not stall at implementation. It stalls at adoption. The missing piece is rarely ideas or tools. It is a setting where teams feel safe to apply judgement, ask the awkward questions, and try things on actual workflows. The model matters, but the contained, governed environment around it is what teams actually need to step into.
