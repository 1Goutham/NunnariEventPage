## The conversation

A special interaction with Padma Shri Dr. Sridhar Vembu, founder of Zoho, hosted on behalf of ANTPOD in the first year of Nunnari Labs. The conversation ranged across the Indian economy, poverty eradication, innovation, entrepreneurship, spirituality, farming and rural development.

## The takeaway

*Know-how, so we control our destiny.* Building deep technical capability in a tier-two city, rather than importing it, is the founding premise of Nunnari Labs. Hearing it from someone who had already done it in Tenkasi mattered.
