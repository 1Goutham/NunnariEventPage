## The event

Tech for Good (T4G) was GDG Coimbatore's edition of Google I/O Extended: a community conference and hackathon on using technology for the betterment of Coimbatore, supported by the Corporation of Coimbatore, the Commissioner of Police, KGiSL Institute of Technology, Thoughtworks, Kovai.co and colleges across the city.

Navaneeth was part of the organising team. The hackathon produced real projects, and the network it built is much of the same one that AI Tamil Nadu draws on today.
