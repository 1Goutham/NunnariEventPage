## The event

Tech for Good (T4G) was GDG Coimbatore's edition of Google I/O Extended: a community conference and hackathon on harnessing technology for the betterment of Coimbatore. The closing note thanked the sponsors, speakers and participants, and tagged the Corporation of Coimbatore, the Commissioner of Police, KGiSL Institute of Technology, Thoughtworks, Kovai.co and colleges across the city.

Navaneeth was one of the organisers.
