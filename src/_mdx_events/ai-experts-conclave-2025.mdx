## The conclave

TFUG Coimbatore and AI Tamil Nadu convened an invite-only meetup for AI experts, researchers and startup founders, hosted at the Nunnari Labs office on the NGP campus.

## The agenda

- **SaaS is dead**: the rise of AI agents.
- **AI adoption index**: ethics and governance.
- **India AI Mission**: opportunities and challenges.
- **AI and the chip wars.**

Two hours of open discussion, no slides. The point of a roundtable is that the people in the room disagree well.
