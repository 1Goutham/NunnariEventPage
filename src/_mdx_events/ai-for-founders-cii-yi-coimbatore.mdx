## The session

Most non-technical founders give up on AI after one chatbot conversation. They have never seen what is underneath. This session for CII Young Indians Coimbatore worked through the platform instead of the demo: Claude Desktop, where multiple agents live inside one application.

## What we built in the room

- **Projects.** Each one a specialist agent with its own role and uploaded knowledge.
- **Connectors.** Live bridges to the founder's real mail, drive, calendar and chat.
- **Background agents.** Multi-step work that runs while you are in another meeting.
- **Scheduled agents.** Wakes up at seven every morning and sends yesterday's report.

The recipe stayed the same throughout: role, knowledge, inputs, outputs. Four ingredients that turn a generic chatbot into something that does a job for the business. None of it is next year. It all ships today.
