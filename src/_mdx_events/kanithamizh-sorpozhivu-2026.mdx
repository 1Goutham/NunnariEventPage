## The session

An invited lecture at கணித்தமிழ் சொற்பொழிவு, hosted by the Tamil Virtual Academy in collaboration with Kongu Arts and Science College, Erode. The title: *செயற்கை நுண்ணறிவும் மாணவர்களுக்கான வாய்ப்புகளும்: 2026*, navigating the 2026 AI landscape and the opportunities it opens for students.

## What we covered

Tamil large language models, the future of language technology, and Tamil's place in an open-world AI ecosystem. From generative AI and agentic systems to building and fine-tuning Tamil LLMs, the opportunity for students today is unprecedented. AI is not just about using tools. It is about building foundational systems, including language models that understand and reason in Tamil.

This is the same thread that runs through our Sovereign AI work: models that belong to the language and the place they serve.
