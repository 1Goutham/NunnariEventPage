## The session

A virtual session for the Atlassian Community in Coimbatore, hosted with Enjo.ai, on where agentic AI fits into Jira workflows. Navaneeth's talk, *The Anatomy of a Reliable Agent*, was followed by a panel with Garvit Bandil of Troopr Labs.

## Three things that make the difference

None of them are the model.

- **Human checkpoints at the right moments**, not everywhere. Approval gates where the cost of a wrong action is high, and nowhere else.
- **Traceability behind every action**, so a team can explain what happened and why after the fact.
- **The discipline to keep a task deterministic** when it should be. Not everything needs an agent, and the ones that do need boundaries.

These are the patterns we run in production at Nunnari Labs and DeepWeaver.ai, and the gap between a slick demo and something a team will actually adopt.
