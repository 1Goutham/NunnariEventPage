## The retreat

ProductCamp 3.0, run by theproductspace.org, is a two-day unconference retreat in Kanthalloor, Kerala. Fifteen people, no agenda, no designations: corporate product managers, startup founders, freelancers and designers, from people who have been coding since the VB.NET days to twenty-two-year-olds who think in AI.

## What happened

In the recap shared after the retreat, Dinesh Kumar Prabakaran wrote that tools were shared left and right. A debate started on whether AI agents and agentic AI are the same thing or entirely different, and it ran until the kitchen closed at three in the morning. Nobody was pretending to have all the answers. People shared their failures, their late-night debugging stories, and the moment a messy idea suddenly made sense.

The talks were good. The real value was in between them, over chai, when someone's problem became everyone's problem to solve.
