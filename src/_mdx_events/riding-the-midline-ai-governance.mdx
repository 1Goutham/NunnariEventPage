## The event

Riding the Midline was DeepWeaver.ai's first AI governance event, run as part of Australia's AI Month 2024 organised by the National AI Centre. The audience spanned federal and state government agencies, healthcare, finance, manufacturing and technology. Navaneeth led the technical discussion as DeepWeaver's AI Practice Head, with Tamilselvan Easwaran and Sakthi Thangavelu bringing the session to life.

## What we showed

- A deep dive into ISO/IEC 42001 and its practical implementation, the standard Nunnari Labs is itself certified against.
- A live demonstration of Shield Gemma for toxic content detection.
- A comparative analysis of enterprise AI governance tools, including Microsoft's Responsible AI toolkit and IBM watsonx.governance.

As AI continues to evolve rapidly, having robust governance frameworks while maintaining innovation is crucial.
