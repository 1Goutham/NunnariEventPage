## The event

Build With AI, Coimbatore was hosted by ML.Cbe, previously TFUG Coimbatore, one of the longest-standing machine learning communities in the region, with AI Tamil Nadu and the support of Google for Developers. Kumaraguru Institutions and the Kumaraguru School of Innovation hosted us.

## The line-up

- **Joinal Ahmed**, Head of AI at Aivar Innovations, on designing insanely fast voice agents.
- **Anubhav Singh**, AI Engineer at Weights & Biases and Google Developer Expert, on running agents faster and cheaper on Cloud Run.
- **Vikram Elango**, Senior GenAI Specialist at AWS, on building scalable production-grade agents.
- **Goutham Nivass**, Senior AI Engineer at Cognizant, on live agentic intelligence with MCP.

## Eight years

This edition marked a milestone in a journey that began eight years ago with nothing but passion and purpose, from TFUG Coimbatore to ML.Cbe. Nunnari Labs grew out of this community, and it is still where our talent pipeline starts.
