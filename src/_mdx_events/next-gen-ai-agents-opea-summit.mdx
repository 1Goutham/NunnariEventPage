## The event

An exclusive summit on the future of enterprise AI, organised by AI Tamil Nadu and supported by Dr. N.G.P. Arts and Science College, Coimbatore.

## The sessions

- **Exploring the Open Platform for Enterprise AI (OPEA)** with Dr. Shriram Vasudevan, Technical Lead at Intel Corporation, a prolific author and educator whose lectures have reached thousands.
- **Agentic AI: multi-agent orchestration with LangGraph** with Maithreyan Kesavan, Technical Manager at Morgan Stanley and co-lead of TFUG Coimbatore.

The summit was organised by AI Tamil Nadu, led by Nunnari Labs.
