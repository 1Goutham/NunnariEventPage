## The session

Kovai Sangamam is the PMI Chennai Chapter's flagship conference in Coimbatore, with more than three hundred project professionals in the room and speakers from Zoho, Microsoft, Thoughtworks, Cognizant and the Indian Air Force. Nunnari Labs was a Diamond Sponsor alongside Zoho and Infinium. Navaneeth's session was titled *Amplifying Human Strengths Through AI*.

## Cognitive surrender

The talk opened with a question most people do not want to answer: how many of you have team members using AI, with no idea what they are feeding into it, directly accepting the output without validating a single thing? The room went quiet.

The backbone was a Wharton paper, *Thinking: Fast, Slow, and Artificial* (Shaw and Nave, 2026), which proposes a System 3 alongside Kahneman's fast and slow thinking: artificial cognition that operates outside your head. The finding that hit hardest is that, given AI access, eighty percent of people followed wrong outputs without questioning them, and their confidence went up. The paper calls this cognitive surrender.

## From surrender to offloading

The rest of the session moved from cognitive surrender to cognitive offloading, where AI extends thinking instead of replacing it: role-based AI workflows, the five-level autonomy spectrum from chatbots to fully autonomous agents, a live demonstration of a thousand person-hours of data sourcing done in thirty minutes, and why AI governance under ISO 42001 is the quality system every organisation now needs.
