## The event

The Bureau of Indian Standards, Coimbatore, marked World Standards Day 2024 with an inaugural address by the Member of Parliament for Coimbatore, Dr. Ganapathy P Rajkumar, and an address by Dr. Ezhilarasi Muthusamy, Principal of Kumaraguru College of Technology. Navaneeth was a guest speaker alongside leaders from Agni Steels, SWELECT Energy Systems and the Coimbatore District All Civil Engineers Association.

## The talk

The future of AI and its impact on manufacturing, education and workforce transformation, then responsible AI: AI governance, the ISO management system for AI, and ISO/IEC 42001 in practice. We spoke about how Nunnari Labs builds trustworthy AI systems by adopting these practices, and about AI Tamil Nadu's work in open-source AI education and research across the state.

Events like these underscore the critical role of standardisation in ensuring responsible and ethical AI development.
