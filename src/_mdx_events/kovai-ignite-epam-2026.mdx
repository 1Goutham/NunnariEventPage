## The session

Kov(AI) Ignite is EPAM Systems' three-day engineering festival at their Coimbatore office. Navaneeth spoke on spec-driven development: how writing clear, testable specs up front changes the way teams build with AI, and why it matters more rather than less as teams lean on coding agents and large language models.

## The production clinic

After the talk we ran a live clinic with five hackathon teams, walking each one through what it would take for their idea to survive real users, real data and real cost.

## What stayed with us

The quality of the work. Many participants were new hires, yet with mentorship from EPAM's senior engineers they had already adopted spec-driven development to build their prototypes. The ideas were sound, the solutions were top class, and most were AI-native by design. You do not often see that from teams this early in their journey.
