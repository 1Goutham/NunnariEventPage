## The talk

A session at Thoughtworks Coimbatore on generative AI and its impact, in the spring of 2023 when most enterprises were still deciding what to make of it. Navaneeth shared the stage with Raju Kandaswamy and Satish Kumar Viswanathan, with Deepthi hosting.

Looking back, it is one of the first public talks where we set out the position we still hold: generative AI is a production engineering problem, not a demo.
