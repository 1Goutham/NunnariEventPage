## The workshop

SaaS.Connect is Tamil Nadu's largest SaaS gathering. On day one, Navaneeth and Sakthivel Senthilkumar ran *Everything Agents*, a workshop on building intelligent agents with n8n aimed at SaaS founders, CXOs and technology leaders.

## Why attend, as the announcement put it

- Learn how to build scalable AI agents.
- Explore workflow automation with n8n.
- Get hands-on insights from industry experts.
