## The conference

XConf India 2023, hosted by Thoughtworks, was a melting pot of technology leaders. Two sessions stood out: *The Top 10 Tech Trends You Need to Know* by Bharani Subramaniam and Vanya Seth, and *The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Showing Up Every Day* by Kishore Nallan, co-founder of Typesense.

## The DeepRacer workshop

Representing KGiSL Educational Institutions, Navaneeth took part in the DeepRacer workshop run by the Thoughtworks software-defined vehicle team.
