## The problem

A state-level prosecuting agency in Australia handles legal documents that must be disclosed — but only after every piece of personally identifiable information is found and redacted. Manual redaction is slow, expensive, and unforgiving: a single missed name is a serious breach.

## What we built

An AI redaction platform that detects and redacts PII across legal document sets — names, addresses, identifiers, and contextual references that simple pattern-matching misses. The system runs on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and is built for legal-grade review: every automated redaction is auditable and human-verifiable before release.

## Outcome

- In production with a state-level prosecutor
- Redaction moved from a manual bottleneck to a reviewed, auditable pipeline
- Delivered in partnership with Oracle on OCI

## Stack

Generative AI and document-AI models for entity detection, audit-trail review tooling, Oracle OCI.
