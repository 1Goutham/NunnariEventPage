## The product

HearSight is a pair of smart glasses for people with low vision, made by HearSight Audio Vision. Nunnari Labs is the AI and engineering partner: perception models, on-device inference, and the pipeline that runs them on the glasses. Every model runs on the glasses themselves — there is no cloud round trip, no connectivity assumption, and no data leaves the wearer.

## What it does

- Object and obstacle detection in real time
- OCR — reading signage, labels and documents aloud
- Face recognition for familiar people
- Navigation assistance and wayfinding
- Payment assistance, fully offline

## Why it matters

Frontier-grade perception, running inside a pair of glasses. Shipping AI into hardware forces discipline that cloud-only teams never build: model compression, latency budgets, thermal limits, offline reliability and safety with no fallback. That discipline is what we bring back into enterprise work.
