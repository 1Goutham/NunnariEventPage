## The problem

Annotating microscopy images by hand is slow, specialist work. Each sample took hours, and throughput was limited by the people available to do it.

## What we built

A vision system trained to annotate geology microscopy images automatically, with specialists reviewing and correcting rather than annotating from scratch.

## Outcome

- **Annotation in seconds rather than hours** per sample
- Specialists moved from manual annotation to review
