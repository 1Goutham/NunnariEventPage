## The problem

Packaging supply chains run on data scattered across systems, formats, and partners. Answering operational questions — where an order stands, why a line item changed, what to reorder and when — meant stitching that data together by hand.

## What we built

AFL 2PACK is an AI-native supply chain intelligence platform. Autonomous agents sit on top of the supply chain data layer: they retrieve, reconcile, and reason over operational data, and surface answers and actions to the teams running the chain. Built and delivered with Oracle.

## Outcome

- An agentic layer over live supply chain operations
- Operational questions answered from data, not from chasing spreadsheets
- Delivered in partnership with Oracle

## Stack

Generative AI, multi-agent orchestration, Oracle OCI.
