## The problem

Finance workflows were manual and repetitive, and the organisation wanted generative AI in everyday work — but only under governance it could stand behind, and only if every member of staff could actually use it.

## What we built

Finance workflows were automated end to end first. The programme then extended into enterprise-wide AI adoption: a governance model, a retained forward-deployed engineer inside the organisation, and training and enablement across every team.

## Outcome

- **5 FTE of capacity freed every week**
- **200 staff onboarded, 100% enablement**
- Governance in place from the first sprint, with a retained FDE keeping adoption moving

## Built on

OCI · Oracle AI Services · Claude · workflow orchestration layer
